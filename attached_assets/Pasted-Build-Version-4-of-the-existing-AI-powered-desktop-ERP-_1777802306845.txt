Build Version 4 of the existing AI-powered desktop ERP application.

==================================================
VERSION 4 GOAL
==================================================

Version 4 is the "Ecosystem, Integrations, and Enterprise Readiness Release".

The purpose of Version 4 is to evolve the product beyond standalone ERP + analytics into a highly connected platform that can:
- integrate with external business tools
- send and receive notifications across channels
- expose secure APIs and webhooks
- support plugin/connectors architecture
- allow custom fields and custom workflows
- prepare for mobile companion access
- support white-label and enterprise deployment options
- enable advanced export/import and data portability
- provide deeper admin controls and observability

The application should now feel like a serious business operating system and integration platform for SMEs, accountants, operations teams, and growing enterprises.
